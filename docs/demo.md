# A Mend desk session

This walkthrough uses fictional details. The live desk requires an agent account; no customer data is published in this repository.

1. **Plan the day.** Set the Cairo shift and pauses in Schedule. Apply the finished day to the rest of the week, then adjust individual days or add a dated exception.
2. **Work the callback queue.** Open a follow-up from Today, record the outcome, and choose a 30-minute, 1-hour, or 2-hour callback when needed. A technician visit recipe reveals its appointment fields only for that case type.
3. **Prepare an escalation.** Open RCC Dispatch, choose Long Island or Nassau Quota, enter the customer name, account, callback number, appointment window, and summary. Account and phone numbers format as you type. Review the rich email and its subject before handoff.
4. **Open Outlook.** The handoff copies formatted mail and opens an Outlook draft; Ctrl+Enter runs the same action. Mend records the prepared draft in history and resets the RCC form for the next request. Sending happens in Outlook.
5. **Keep a case.** Save an RCC request or follow-up as a Case when it needs ongoing attention. Its changes remain in a private history.

Mend distinguishes **prepared**, **sent**, and **confirmed** work. It does not infer a sent email or confirmed appointment from opening a draft.
